Strategy Rooted in Connection

How I Approach Strategy

I start by listening. I notice what people need before they say it, then turn that insight into direction. My approach combines four things:

  • Consumer behavior: understanding the emotions and motivations behind what people choose.
  • Visual storytelling: translating those insights into imagery and narratives people recognize themselves in.
  • Analytics: using data to test what resonates and refine the work.
  • Strategy: connecting it all to clear goals for the brand.
