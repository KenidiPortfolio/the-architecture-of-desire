Where Fashion Meets Identity

I’m an aspiring fashion brand strategist who believes the best brands are built on how people feel, not just what they buy. From watching customers bring in family heirlooms at a jewelry store to seeing clients transform in a personal styling fitting, I’ve learned that fashion is where memory, identity, and confidence meet. I approach strategy by listening first: noticing what people need before they say it, then using consumer insight, visual storytelling, and data to build brands people see themselves in. My goal is to help fashion and luxury brands create lasting, personal connections with the audiences they serve
